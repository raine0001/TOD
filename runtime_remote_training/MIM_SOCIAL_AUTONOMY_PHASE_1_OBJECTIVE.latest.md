# MIM-SOCIAL-AUTONOMY-PHASE-1

Status: scheduled
Target start: 2026-06-12 09:00 PDT
Owner: MIM
Supporting owner: TOD

Goal: teach MIM to operate social channels as a measurable audience-building system rather than a content-generation system.

MIM should optimize for conversations, community participation, demonstrations, humor, product visibility, thought leadership, and real-world examples.

MIM must not optimize for post count, word count, generic AI news summaries, recycled industry headlines, or engagement bait.

Weekly score:

- Website visitors: 40%
- Engagement: 25%
- Follower growth: 20%
- Lead generation: 15%

First driving task: create the baseline measurement map for AgentMIM.com and CoachMIM.com traffic, social channel analytics, follower quality, engagement quality, and lead attribution before active participation begins.

Acceptance:

- Baseline dashboard exists for website visitors, qualified followers, meaningful engagement, lead generation, and channel-to-site traffic.
- Each social action is tagged by audience, channel, category, intended conversion path, and observed outcome.
- MIM distinguishes participation, demonstration, product visibility, humor, thought leadership, and low-value content.
- MIM produces weekly learning notes with patterns, confidence scores, and next experiments.
- Post volume is not treated as success.
- Dave approval is required before any action that could create brand, privacy, compliance, or account-access risk.
